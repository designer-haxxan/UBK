Admissions

How to apply

The whole process takes about two weeks from application to offer.

Start your application

Five steps

From application to offer in about two weeks. Here is exactly what happens at each stage.

01
Choose your track

Review the four skill tracks — Graphic Design, eCommerce, Digital Marketing and Video Editing — and pick the one that matches where you want your career to go.

02
Fill in the application

Complete the online form. It takes about ten minutes and asks for your background, your goals and your preferred intake date.

03
Complete the skills exercise

We send you a short practical exercise related to your chosen track. There is nothing to study — we want to see how you think and solve problems.

04
Attend a short interview

A fifteen-minute video call with one of our mentors. We want to understand your motivation and make sure UBK is right for you.

05
Receive your offer

Successful applicants get an offer within five working days. Accept it, choose your funding option and you are in.

Contact Us

Who can enrol at UBK?

Any madrasa graduate, and anyone else willing to learn. No prior computer experience is required — every track begins from the very first step.

Do I get a certificate after completion?

Yes. Every student who completes the attendance, projects and final assessment receives a UBK certificate.

Do I really pay nothing on a scholarship?

Correct. Scholarship students pay no tuition fee at all — the full course, material and mentor sessions are covered.

Are the courses beginner friendly?

Yes. Lessons start with the basics and mentors support you one-to-one until you are confident.
