Who This Track Suits

This track is for anyone who wants to help businesses grow online — whether you are drawn to social media, advertising or content. You do not need a marketing background; we teach every platform and strategy from the ground up.

By the end of twelve weeks you will run paid ad campaigns, manage social media accounts, write marketing copy and read analytics. You will leave with campaign results you can show employers and clients.
